## How the keys are marked

Each key gives a model answer, not the only acceptable one. Award full marks when
the mechanism is present, even if the wording differs. Award nothing for a
definition that is not applied to the question asked.

Section D answers are marked element by element against the listed criteria. A
polished answer that misses the central risk scores lower than a plain answer that
names it.

If your score is below 18/30 on a bank, reread the module before doing its lab.
The lab will not repair a missing concept, and the later mini-project assumes it.
